# Playful Pause

**Where whimsy takes flight — quick, joyful activities under three minutes. No streaks, no pressure.**

🔗 **Live:** [playful-pause.vercel.app](https://playful-pause.vercel.app)

---

## What it is

A small break-taking app built against a single constraint: **it must never guilt you.**

Most wellness and habit apps run on streaks, badges, and daily targets — mechanics that convert a missed day into a small failure. Playful Pause deliberately has none of that. There is no streak counter, no progress bar, no notification asking where you've been. You pick how you're feeling, you get a short activity, and nothing is tracked afterward.

## The design problem

The interesting constraint here is motivational, not technical. Remove streaks and you remove the standard engagement mechanic — so the interface has to carry the invitation on its own.

The answer was tone. Hand-drawn doodles rather than iconography, an emotion-first entry point instead of a menu of activities, and copy written to sound like a friend suggesting something rather than an app assigning it. You choose a feeling; the app meets you there.

## How it works

Pick an emotion tile, get a matching activity card — each one designed to finish in under three minutes. That's the entire flow. The brevity is the point: a break long enough to reset, short enough that there's no reason to talk yourself out of it.
